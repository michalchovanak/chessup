# Q: Why is ChessUp a strong WebMCP use case and what are its product risks?

## Answer

ChessUp is a strong WebMCP candidate because the human and agent share a live chess board, structured game state, annotations, event history, and deterministic validation. Its strongest differentiation is converting the player's own mistakes into personalized validated drills. Key risks are brittle pull-based live sparring, lack of engine-grounded evaluation, an event-draining read tool marked read-only, and inconsistent tool counts in documentation. Reposition the hero demo around play versus bot, agent review, and mistake-to-drill adaptation; use live sparring only as a secondary feature.
